Overview

What this is, and isn’t

SELF’s discovery is a guidance aid. It organises your own answers into a clear picture and shows which draft career profiles they sit closest to. It is not a diagnosis, a clinical test or a prediction of success.

Nothing is hidden: every number below is produced by a formula you can follow, and your report shows the same workings for your own answers.

Step 1

Assessment criteria

Interests · 6 statements. A six-type interest model used by career counsellors worldwide: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional, one statement each, rated 1–5 (not at all to very much).
Aptitude · 3 questions. One each on numerical, logical and verbal reasoning, each with one correct answer. Scored right or wrong, so no self-rating is involved.
Work values · 1 choice. From Creativity, Stability, Impact on people, Learning & growth, Independence and Teamwork, you choose the two that matter most to you.

The chat with Meera (your name and two quick questions) is for context only. It is not scored. In total the assessment is 10 questions.

Step 2

How the scores are calculated

Interest type score = (your rating − 1) ÷ 4 × 100
Aptitude area score = 100 if correct, otherwise 0
Value score = 100 if you chose it, otherwise 0
Interest fit = 100 − average gap between your 6 interest scores and the career’s 6 emphasis levels
Aptitude fit = Σ (career weight × your area score)
Values fit = average of your value scores on the career’s 3 key values, so 0, 33, 67 or 100
Overall fit = 0.5 × interest fit + 0.3 × aptitude fit + 0.2 × values fit
Interests carry the most weight because they are the strongest long-run signal for satisfaction. Aptitude and values adjust the ranking. The 50/30/20 split is a design choice, not a research constant, and it is shown so you can judge it.

Worked example

An Artistic rating of 4 → (4 − 1) ÷ 4 × 100 = 75.
Your interests [30, 60, 85, 40, 50, 20] vs a career’s [10, 55, 90, 50, 40, 30]: gaps 20, 5, 5, 10, 10, 10 → average 10 → interest fit 90.
Weights .25 / .40 / .35 × your scores 100 / 0 / 100 = 60.
You chose 2 of the career’s 3 key values → values fit 67.
Overall = .5 × 90 + .3 × 60 + .2 × 67 = 76.

Step 4

How to read your results

Interest code = your top three interest letters. Ties are ordered R, I, A, S, E, C, and the report says when that happens. With one rating per type, ties are likely.
Close calls. If the top three careers are within 5 points of each other, the report says so instead of implying a winner. Differences that small are well within what a 10-question assessment can resolve.
No right or wrong labels. Scores describe fit with a profile, never your worth or ability.

Limits

Limits and fairness

  • Interests and values are self-reported, so they reflect how you see yourself today and can change.
  • This is a short form: one rating per interest type and one question per aptitude area. Ties between interest types are common, and aptitude is indicative at best, not a measurement of ability.
  • This is not a validated psychometric instrument, and we make no claims about predictive accuracy.
  • Career profiles are drafts pending expert review. Only 8 careers are covered, so a good fit elsewhere is possible.
  • The chat with Meera is not scored.

If your result feels off, trust that feeling and talk to a counsellor.
